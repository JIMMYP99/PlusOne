Plus One

These files are the whole app. Put them on any free static host and open the link on your phone.

GitHub Pages (free, permanent)
1. Sign in at github.com and create a new public repository, e.g. plus-one.
2. Upload every file in this folder to it (Add file > Upload files). Keep index.html at the top level.
3. In the repository go to Settings > Pages. Under Build and deployment choose "Deploy from a branch",
   pick the main branch and "/ (root)", then Save.
4. After a minute the page shows your link, like https://YOURNAME.github.io/plus-one/

On an iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
3. Open it from the home screen. It runs full screen and works offline once it has loaded once.

Your numbers are stored on the phone only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Anyone you send the link to gets their own empty copy of the app, with their own numbers.
To give someone your exercise setup, open Settings in the app, tap Copy data, send them the text,
and they paste it into Settings > Import data.
